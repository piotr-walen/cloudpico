# BME280 Pin Connections for Raspberry Pi Pico

## Standard Connections (Default I2C Pins)

 * PIN CONNECTIONS (Raspberry Pi Pico):
 * ====================================
 * Pico Pin  | GPIO  | Function | BME280 Pin
 * ----------|-------|----------|------------
 * Pin 21    | GP16  | SDA      | SDA
 * Pin 22    | GP17  | SCL      | SCL
 * Pin 36    | 3V3   | Power    | VCC/VIN
 * Pin 38    | GND   | Ground   | GND
